## Scope and limits

The public sitemap contained 58 blog URLs on 2026-11-11. A read-only fetch of each returned HTTP 200; each rendered route had a title, meta description, and H1. This is a route/metadata inventory, not a complete editorial fact-check. Search Console metrics below are included only for pages with figures in the available audit extract. Other per-page impressions, clicks, positions, query-to-page joins, and backlinks were not available in that extract. Do not interpret missing figures as zero.

The shared blog template currently links related articles and sends its final call to action to the generic `/services` index. It does not choose a marketplace-specific service page. Local code now selects the relevant service route from the article topic, and service pages have contextual supporting-guide links. These template changes take effect only after deployment.

No article should be deleted, noindexed, consolidated, or redirected solely from this inventory. Check complete Search Console page/query data, backlinks, and editorial ownership first. The three older articles below rendered fewer than three H2 elements in the live route check; review their heading hierarchy and substantive content before updating.
